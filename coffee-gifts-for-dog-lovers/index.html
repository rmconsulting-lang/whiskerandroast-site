Coffee gifts for dog lovers

These are printed gifts for someone who likes dogs and a cup of coffee. Whisker & Roast does not sell coffee beans, ground coffee, or a drink. You are choosing a mug, a canvas tote, or a throw pillow.

The mugs are the Victorian dog coffee mug, the cozy winter beagle mug, and the personalized Christmas mug. The sample photo on that Christmas mug shows the name Biscuit. The totes and pillows use the Victorian dog-and-coffee artwork, including the Christmas sweater tote (the dog is holding a cup) and the Christmas barista pillow. The Thanksgiving dog mug, “Here for the Turkey,” stays on the main catalog: it is a holiday mug, and the picture is the turkey table rather than coffee. Dog Christmas ornaments and pet bandanas have their own pages. Prices are the catalog prices on each card, with free shipping.
